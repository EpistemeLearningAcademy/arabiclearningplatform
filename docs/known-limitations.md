# Known limitations (v2.0 foundation)
- Only Level 1 vocabulary (36 words) and the alphabet exist. Levels 2 and 3 are shown as "Not built yet".
- Alphabet module is partial: 28 letters, written forms and a recognition quiz. No sounds, vowels, tracing or letter-position drills yet. Letter names are standard but unreviewed.
- No grammar, reading, writing, listening, conversation or sentence-builder modules yet.
- No recorded audio. The Listen button uses the device's Arabic voice if present; otherwise it says so.
- No achievements/badges, weekly challenges or certificates yet. XP, streak and mastery do work.
- No teacher/parent portal. Profiles are local to one device and are not secure accounts.
- Arabic content and Arabic UI strings need review by a qualified Arabic teacher.
- Not tested in a real browser or on Android in this environment. See docs/testing-guide.md.
- Lessons: 64 (13 Beginner, 21 Intermediate, 30 Advanced). Beginner: letter positions, short and long vowels, sukūn, shaddah, tanwīn. Intermediate: definite article and sun/moon letters, gender, pronouns, demonstratives, dual and plural, verbs (past, present, sentence types, لَا), adjectives, prepositions, possessives, question words. Advanced: sentence structures (sound feminine plural by case, كان and إنّ with their sisters in outline, relative pronouns, numbers 3-5), case endings, roots and patterns, verb conjugation, weak verbs, derived forms, particles, future, moods, imperative, passive. All are unreviewed by a native-speaker teacher.
- "Lesson complete" is a self-marked completion, separate from the practice score; a lesson does not set item mastery.
- Not built yet: tracing, lessons on the six non-joining letters in words, hamza and tāʾ marbūṭa, and the other letters' position drill for non-joiners (the drill covers the 22 letters that join forward). Word examples in the long-vowel lessons (bāb, fīl, nūr) are dictionary forms.
- Intermediate covers nouns, pronouns, sun/moon letters, dual and plural only. Past-tense negation, jussive/“lam”, and verbs for you/we/they are not built. Verb lessons cover 3rd-person he/she and “I” only. The dual -ayni and sound-plural -īna forms are mentioned but case endings are not yet taught. Level 3 is not built.
- Reading Library: 8 short graded passages (home, people, animals, family, the cat; Advanced: case endings, the student's day, passive voice), all fully vowelled and built from words taught in the lessons. No stories, no Classical Arabic enrichment passages yet (the passage format supports a `classical: true` label). Passage questions are multiple choice. The 🔊 button uses the device voice only.
- Achievements: 22 badges, each worth 20 XP once. The Listening Star needs 2 passed (70%+) listening quizzes. Reading Champion is earned by passing (70%+) any reading comprehension quiz. Letter Master (6 letters mastered) is our own definition of a "letter group"; edit `content/shared/achievements.json` to change it.
- Listening lab: plays words with the browser's speech synthesis only. It needs an Arabic voice on the device; without one the quiz is disabled with a message rather than pretending to play. Synthetic voices can mispronounce vowelled Arabic, so this is not verified native pronunciation. There is no recorded audio, no repeat-after-me and no sentence listening yet. Listening quizzes use the 36 vocabulary words only.
- Sentence builder: 8 sentences, 6 per round. The English meaning is shown (not translated into Arabic, because that would give away the answer). Some sentences accept two word orders (verb-first and subject-first). Case endings are not taught, so sentences are given with the endings shown.
- Level 3 (Advanced) is only started: case endings for regular singular nouns, dual and sound masculine plural (6 lessons), plus roots and three word patterns (2 lessons), and one reading passage with case endings. Not built: weak verbs, derived verb forms (I–X), the sound feminine plural and diptotes, particles, complex sentences, essay writing, longer passages, advanced listening and independent conversation.
- Classical Arabic enrichment is deliberately NOT included yet: it needs verified texts and scholarly review, and I will not invent attributions or meanings. The passage format already supports `classical: true` so such texts will be labelled when added.
- Some multiple-choice options that are Arabic words inside English/Arabic option objects are shown as plain text (left-to-right); they display correctly but are not styled as Arabic script.
- Sentence-builder sentences are not gated by lesson progress, so a learner may see sentences using grammar they have not studied yet.
- Advanced verbs: conjugation of one sound verb (كَتَبَ) in the past and present, the three weak-verb types with one model verb each, and forms I, II, III, IV, V and X by example only. Forms VI-IX, other weak-verb patterns, jussive/subjunctive moods, passive voice, and the imperative are not built.
- Particles: only وَ، ثُمَّ، أَوْ، سَـ، سَوْفَ، لَنْ. Essay writing, longer passages, independent conversation and text analysis (Level 3 items) are not built.
- Writing studio: free tracing of letters (all four forms) and the 36 vocabulary words on a canvas. It measures only how much of the guide you covered and how much of your ink stayed on it. It cannot judge stroke order or shape, there is no stroke-order animation (no verified stroke data), and a canvas cannot be drawn on with a keyboard.
- Badges: 22. Tracing badge needs 10 checked attempts.
- Not built: recorded audio and repeat-after-me, an in-app update prompt, class/cloud features.
- Parent & teacher centre: LOCAL ONLY. It lists every learner profile stored on this browser, shows summaries, lets an adult set assignments (a lesson, a reading passage or a sentence-builder round, with optional due date and note) and print or export (CSV) a learner report. There is no teacher account, no password or PIN (anyone holding the device can open it), no sync between devices, no messaging and no way for a teacher on another device to see a learner's progress except by the learner exporting a backup file. An assignment counts as done when its target is completed/passed now; it does not check whether that happened after the assignment was set. Reports are generated on the device and are not verified by any server.
- Weekly challenges: 6 challenge templates, 3 chosen per week (Monday-Sunday, device time), each worth 30 XP once. Challenge progress counts only lessons completed after this version (older completions have no date) and quiz attempts/days recorded after this version. Reward is awarded the next time progress is saved.
- Certificates: 6, each earned when every lesson in a set of modules is completed and its practice passed with 70% or more. They are printed from the browser (Print -> Save as PDF) and state clearly that they are issued by the app on this device and are not an accredited qualification. Anyone who edits the exported backup could forge progress; do not treat certificates as proof of attainment.
- Advanced grammar now also covers: the three moods of the present verb, negation in all tenses, the imperative and negative imperative, the passive voice (past and present, regular verbs), derived forms VI-IX by example, and a review. NOT covered: moods for forms like يَكْتُبُونَ (final nūn dropping), passive of weak verbs, the imperative of weak verbs, diptotes, conditional sentences, the other sisters of كان and إنّ (only كَانَ, لَيْسَ, إِنَّ, لٰكِنَّ are taught), relative pronouns for plural/dual, numbers 6 and above and 11-99, ordinal numbers. These are real parts of Arabic grammar that remain unwritten.
- Guided writing and copy typing: copy typing compares what you type with the model sentence (letters only by default, vowel marks optional). Guided writing checks only sentence count and that required words appear in the text. It cannot judge grammar, spelling, meaning or style, so a learner can pass the checklist with poor Arabic. Four prompts exist (two Intermediate, one Advanced paragraph, one Advanced short essay). Drafts are saved on the device. Typing Arabic needs an Arabic keyboard on the device.
- Reading passages are written by the app author from known vocabulary and are short and simple. There are no authentic texts, stories, or Classical Arabic passages.
- ALL Arabic content (lessons, passages, prompts, interface strings) was written without review by a native-speaker teacher. Do not treat any of it as verified until a qualified Arabic teacher has reviewed it; the `review` field in the content files says "unreviewed".
