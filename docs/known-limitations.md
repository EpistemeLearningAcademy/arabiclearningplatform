# Known limitations (v2.0 foundation)
- Only Level 1 vocabulary (36 words) and the alphabet exist. Levels 2 and 3 are shown as "Not built yet".
- Alphabet module is partial: 28 letters, written forms and a recognition quiz. No sounds, vowels, tracing or letter-position drills yet. Letter names are standard but unreviewed.
- No grammar, reading, writing, listening, conversation or sentence-builder modules yet.
- No recorded audio. The Listen button uses the device's Arabic voice if present; otherwise it says so.
- No achievements/badges, weekly challenges or certificates yet. XP, streak and mastery do work.
- No teacher/parent portal. Profiles are local to one device and are not secure accounts.
- Arabic content and Arabic UI strings need review by a qualified Arabic teacher.
- Not tested in a real browser or on Android in this environment. See docs/testing-guide.md.
- Lessons: 52 (13 Beginner, 21 Intermediate, 18 Advanced). Intermediate includes verbs-and-sentences and adjectives/prepositions/possessives/questions units ( including a verbs-and-sentences unit: past, present, verbal/nominal sentences, لَا negation, review; before that: nouns/pronouns unit plus sun and moon letters, dual, sound plurals, broken plurals and a review; the first 5 are: definite article, gender, pronouns, “this”, review; plus 13 Beginner: letter positions; fatḥa, kasra, ḍamma, review; ā, ī, ū, review; sukūn, shaddah, tanwīn, review). Practice is multiple-choice. Explanations, objectives and feedback have English and Arabic versions, but the Arabic wording was written without a native-speaker review. No guided/independent-practice split or per-lesson assessment bank yet.
- "Lesson complete" is a self-marked completion, separate from the practice score; a lesson does not set item mastery.
- Not built yet: tracing, lessons on the six non-joining letters in words, hamza and tāʾ marbūṭa, and the other letters' position drill for non-joiners (the drill covers the 22 letters that join forward). Word examples in the long-vowel lessons (bāb, fīl, nūr) are dictionary forms.
- Intermediate covers nouns, pronouns, sun/moon letters, dual and plural only. Past-tense negation, jussive/“lam”, and verbs for you/we/they are not built. Verb lessons cover 3rd-person he/she and “I” only. The dual -ayni and sound-plural -īna forms are mentioned but case endings are not yet taught. Level 3 is not built.
- Reading Library: 4 short graded passages (home, people, animals, and one Advanced passage with case endings), all fully vowelled and built from words taught in the lessons. No stories, no Classical Arabic enrichment passages yet (the passage format supports a `classical: true` label). Passage questions are multiple choice. The 🔊 button uses the device voice only.
- Achievements: 20 badges, each worth 20 XP once. The Listening Star needs 2 passed (70%+) listening quizzes. Reading Champion is earned by passing (70%+) any reading comprehension quiz. Letter Master (6 letters mastered) is our own definition of a "letter group"; edit `content/shared/achievements.json` to change it.
- Listening lab: plays words with the browser's speech synthesis only. It needs an Arabic voice on the device; without one the quiz is disabled with a message rather than pretending to play. Synthetic voices can mispronounce vowelled Arabic, so this is not verified native pronunciation. There is no recorded audio, no repeat-after-me and no sentence listening yet. Listening quizzes use the 36 vocabulary words only.
- Sentence builder: 8 sentences, 6 per round. The English meaning is shown (not translated into Arabic, because that would give away the answer). Some sentences accept two word orders (verb-first and subject-first). Case endings are not taught, so sentences are given with the endings shown.
- Level 3 (Advanced) is only started: case endings for regular singular nouns, dual and sound masculine plural (6 lessons), plus roots and three word patterns (2 lessons), and one reading passage with case endings. Not built: weak verbs, derived verb forms (I–X), the sound feminine plural and diptotes, particles, complex sentences, essay writing, longer passages, advanced listening and independent conversation.
- Classical Arabic enrichment is deliberately NOT included yet: it needs verified texts and scholarly review, and I will not invent attributions or meanings. The passage format already supports `classical: true` so such texts will be labelled when added.
- Some multiple-choice options that are Arabic words inside English/Arabic option objects are shown as plain text (left-to-right); they display correctly but are not styled as Arabic script.
- Sentence-builder sentences are not gated by lesson progress, so a learner may see sentences using grammar they have not studied yet.
- Advanced verbs: conjugation of one sound verb (كَتَبَ) in the past and present, the three weak-verb types with one model verb each, and forms I, II, III, IV, V and X by example only. Forms VI-IX, other weak-verb patterns, jussive/subjunctive moods, passive voice, and the imperative are not built.
- Particles: only وَ، ثُمَّ، أَوْ، سَـ، سَوْفَ، لَنْ. Essay writing, longer passages, independent conversation and text analysis (Level 3 items) are not built.
- Writing studio: free tracing of letters (all four forms) and the 36 vocabulary words on a canvas. It measures only how much of the guide you covered and how much of your ink stayed on it. It cannot judge stroke order or shape, there is no stroke-order animation (no verified stroke data), and a canvas cannot be drawn on with a keyboard.
- Badges: 20. Tracing badge needs 10 checked attempts.
- Not built: parent/teacher centre, weekly challenges, completion certificates, recorded audio and repeat-after-me, an in-app update prompt.
