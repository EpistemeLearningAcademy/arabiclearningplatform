# Known limitations (v2.0 foundation)
- Only Level 1 vocabulary (36 words) and the alphabet exist. Levels 2 and 3 are shown as "Not built yet".
- Alphabet module is partial: 28 letters, written forms and a recognition quiz. No sounds, vowels, tracing or letter-position drills yet. Letter names are standard but unreviewed.
- No grammar, reading, writing, listening, conversation or sentence-builder modules yet.
- No recorded audio. The Listen button uses the device's Arabic voice if present; otherwise it says so.
- No achievements/badges, weekly challenges or certificates yet. XP, streak and mastery do work.
- No teacher/parent portal. Profiles are local to one device and are not secure accounts.
- Arabic content and Arabic UI strings need review by a qualified Arabic teacher.
- Not tested in a real browser or on Android in this environment. See docs/testing-guide.md.
- Lessons: 23 (10 Intermediate: nouns/pronouns unit plus sun and moon letters, dual, sound plurals, broken plurals and a review; the first 5 are: definite article, gender, pronouns, “this”, review; plus 13 Beginner: letter positions; fatḥa, kasra, ḍamma, review; ā, ī, ū, review; sukūn, shaddah, tanwīn, review). Practice is multiple-choice. Explanations, objectives and feedback have English and Arabic versions, but the Arabic wording was written without a native-speaker review. No guided/independent-practice split or per-lesson assessment bank yet.
- "Lesson complete" is a self-marked completion, separate from the practice score; a lesson does not set item mastery.
- Not built yet: tracing, lessons on the six non-joining letters in words, hamza and tāʾ marbūṭa, and the other letters' position drill for non-joiners (the drill covers the 22 letters that join forward). Word examples in the long-vowel lessons (bāb, fīl, nūr) are dictionary forms.
- Intermediate covers nouns, pronouns, sun/moon letters, dual and plural only. Verbs, prepositions, adjectives, possessives and sentence building are not built. The dual -ayni and sound-plural -īna forms are mentioned but case endings are not yet taught. Level 3 is not built.
- Reading Library: 3 short graded passages (home, people, animals), all fully vowelled and built from words taught in the lessons. No stories, no Classical Arabic enrichment passages yet (the passage format supports a `classical: true` label). Passage questions are multiple choice. The 🔊 button uses the device voice only.
- Achievements: 11 badges, each worth 20 XP once. The Listening Star is not included because listening assessments do not exist yet. Reading Champion is earned by passing (70%+) any reading comprehension quiz. Letter Master (6 letters mastered) is our own definition of a "letter group"; edit `content/shared/achievements.json` to change it.
