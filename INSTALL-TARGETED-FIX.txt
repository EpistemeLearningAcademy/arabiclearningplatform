What this patch does:
- Replaces the six misassigned body-part images with separate arm, leg, foot, finger, hair and teeth images.
- Corrects the household image mapping: window, plate and spoon replace the sofa, clock and mirror mismatches. Chair remains a chair; no sofa image is assigned to the Chair vocabulary.
- Adds Purple, Pink, Brown, Grey (Ash) and Orange with colour swatches and Arabic vocabulary.
- Makes the Listening Lab available even when Android reports no Arabic voice before the user starts.
- Adds a test-pronunciation button and improves speechSynthesis resume/voice refresh handling.
- Bumps the service-worker cache to kalimati-v2-22 so the browser fetches this release.

NOT CHANGED:
- Learner profiles, IndexedDB/localStorage progress, authentication, other lessons, animals, fruits, numbers, dashboard layout, and unrelated content.

After upload:
1. Commit the changes to GitHub.
2. Wait for GitHub Pages to finish deploying.
3. Open the app online once and reload. If an older screen remains, close the app tab and reopen it.
4. In Listening Lab, tap “Test Arabic pronunciation”. If the device has no Arabic TTS voice, install/download an Arabic voice in Android's Text-to-speech settings and retry. Browser speech availability depends on the device's installed speech service.
